# Lee et al. 2019, "A new alvarezsaurid dinosaur from the Nemegt Formation of Mongolia"

Lee, Park, Lee, Kim, Lü, Barsbold & Tsogtbaatar. *Scientific Reports* 9:15493. doi 10.1038/s41598-019-52021-y. Not cited by Makovicky; *Nemegtonykus* is in its tree.

Files: `papers/s41598-019-52021-y.pdf`, 12 pages. `papers/s41598-019-52021-y (1).pdf` is the same file (same MD5).

## Claim

- *Nemegtonykus citus*, MPC-D 100/203, plus a referred specimen and a cf. *Mononykus*. Found together with several oviraptorids in under 0.5 m2 at Altan Uul III.
- Holotype about 3.4 kg. Qin 2021 Table 1: 3.31.
- Parvicursorine, in a polytomy with other Gobi taxa.
- "The presence of three alvarezsaurid individuals in the same locality indicates that the abundance of alvarezsaurids have been greatly underestimated in the Nemegt dinosaur faunas."
- The origin question, as it stood in 2019: "It was initially suggested that alvarezsaurs originated in South America, but recent discoveries of basal alvarezsaurs in China support an Asian origin of this clade."
