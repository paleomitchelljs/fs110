# Xing et al. 2024, "Deinonychosaur trackways in southeastern China record a possible giant troodontid"

Xing, Niu, Lockley, Romilio, Deng & Persons. *iScience* 27:109598 (2024). doi 10.1016/j.isci.2024.109598. Open access.

File: `papers/xingetal2024.pdf`, 12 pages. Read in full.

## Claim

- Longxiang tracksite, Shaxian Fm. (~96 Ma), Fujian. Twelve didactyl tracks in two forms: small (10.9 cm, *Velociraptorichnus* isp.) and large (36.4 cm, new ichnotaxon *Fujianipus yingliangi*). The large ones are one trackway, LXIU-T3: five tracks over 14.3 m, with a 9 m gap after the first.
- Maker: "The reduced form of digit IV, relative to digit III, indicates that F. yingliangi is a probable troodontid." The small tracks, with equal digits III and IV, are read as eudromaeosaurian.
- Size: hip height 197 cm with Tsukiji's troodontid ratio, "likely an overestimation"; 156 cm with the dromaeosaurid ratio; 184 cm with a general theropod equation; "confidently assumed to have exceeded 1.5 m." Body length ~5 m from a digit-to-body ratio averaged across groups. Gigantism "evolved independently at least four times within the Deinonychosauria."
- Alternative maker named and set aside: *Achillobator*, a large dromaeosaurid of overlapping age. Their limit: "it is an assumption of this study that A. giganticus conformed to the typical pedal morphology of other dromaeosaurids and did not have a digit IV that was reduced relative to digit III," because its foot is unknown.
- Speed ~7.3 km/h, walking or trotting. "The size and speed estimations presented here are based on small sample sizes."

A clean case of the trackmaker problem: the clade comes from one toe ratio, the size from a ratio that depends on the clade, and the main alternative is excluded by assumption. Pairs with Tsukiji's dromaeosaurid-or-troodontid speeds.

Pairs with [Tsukiji et al. 2021](tsukiji-2021.md), [Li et al. 2008](li-2008.md), [Xing et al. 2018](xing-2018.md).
