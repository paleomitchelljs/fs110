# Tsukiji et al. 2021, "First discovery of a deinonychosaur trackway from the lower Upper Cretaceous of southeastern China"

Tsukiji, Jin, Du, Azuma, Hattori, Nakada, Nakayama, Noda & Zheng. *Cretaceous Research* 125:104874 (2021). doi 10.1016/j.cretres.2021.104874.

File: `papers/tsukiji2021.pdf`, 10 pages. Read in full.

## Claim

- YWM 08381, Jinhua Fm. (Cenomanian or later), Yiwu, Zhejiang. One trackway, nine tracks, six consecutive. *Velociraptorichnus sichuanensis*.
- Point: the best *Velociraptorichnus* for speed and gait, since earlier ones were "based only on two consecutive tracks."
- The trackmaker problem: two-toed tracks could be a dromaeosaurid or a troodontid, and the two have different leg proportions. Hip height from foot length uses h/FL = 4.32 for dromaeosaurids, 5.47 for troodontids (averaged from five dromaeosaurid and two troodontid skeletons).
- Result: "6.1 km/h for dromaeosaurids and 4.6 km/h for troodontids, both of which indicate that this trackway was made by a walking gait."
- Re-running other two-toed trackways: for several, "the estimated gait of the trackmaker differs depending on either using dromaeosaurid or troodontid h/FL values" (trot vs walk, run vs trot). "establishment of a criterion to identify whether the trackmaker is a dromeosaurid or troodontid is required."
- Mentions Xing et al. 2018a, "Multiple parallel deinonychosaurian trackways" from the Dasheng Group of Shandong: a second parallel-trackway site, not in the folder.

A useful caution for the tracks line: the maker's clade is an assumption, and changing it changes the result. One trackway, so it says nothing about groups.

Pairs with [Li et al. 2008](li-2008.md).
