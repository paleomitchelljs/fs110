# Amiot et al. 2017, "δ18O-derived incubation temperatures of oviraptorosaur eggs"

Amiot, Wang X., Wang S., Lécuyer, Mazin, Mo, Flandrois, Fourel, Wang X.-L., Xu, Zhang & Zhou. *Palaeontology* 60(5):633-647 (2017). doi 10.1111/pala.12311.

File: `papers/amiot2017.pdf`, 15 pages. Read the abstract, methods (model assumptions), results and discussion; tables and figures viewed.

## Claim

- Seven oviraptorosaur eggs with embryo bones, Nanxiong Fm., Jiangxi (the formation of Bi 2021's clutch). Two Nile crocodile eggs measured as a living control; five bird species from the literature.
- Logic: eggshell δ18O records the egg water; embryo bone grows from that water at the egg's temperature; a growth model run at 30-40 °C predicts the bone value for each temperature.
- Result: "oviraptorosaurs incubated their eggs within a 35–40°C range, similar to extant birds and compatible with the known active brooding behaviour of these theropod dinosaurs."
- What survives: two eggs (IVPP V14723.2, V14723.3) fit directly. NHMG10876 fits only "by lowering the amount of egg water loss" by two thirds. The other four "had embryo δ18Op values completely out of the expected range, corresponding to unlikely incubation temperatures far above 45°C"; "Pending additional analyses, specimens GMV2212, IVPP V20182, V20183 and V20184 are considered to have been affected by diagenetic alteration, and will not be considered further." Their microstructure looked well preserved anyway.
- Assumptions named in the model: egg mass from a bird egg-to-body allometry with dinosaur egg mass doubled (two oviducts); water loss 13% of egg mass; precocial-bird embryo growth (Lee 2016); a +2.5 °C rise late in incubation as in chickens and ostriches.
- Side results: adults 36-140 kg; incubation 44-56 days by Lee's bird-based model, shorter than tooth-line estimates for ornithischians (3-6 months).
- Caveat in the abstract: "Provided that both eggshell and embryo bones preserved their original oxygen isotope compositions."

How much material: seven eggs, two clean fits. Discarding the four that gave impossible values is reasonable, but it also means the method's own check (looks pristine) failed four times out of seven. Bi 2021 used this method on its clutch, with Amiot as a co-author, so the two temperature estimates share a method and a team; they are not independent lines. The 44-56 day incubation is in tension with Varricchio 2018's 74 days for *Troodon*.
