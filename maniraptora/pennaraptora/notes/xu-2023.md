## Claim

- *Fujianvenator prodigiosus*, IVPP V31985, Nanyuan Fm., Zhenghe, Fujian. Tithonian, 149.9-150.2 Ma. "one of the stratigraphically youngest and geographically southernmost Jurassic avialans." Subadult, ~641 g, "around the size of a koklass pheasant." Name: "'prodigiosus', bizarre (Latin), referring to the odd hindlimb morphology."
- The legs: "the hyperelongated tibia is twice as long as the femur, markedly exceeding the ratio in all other known Mesozoic theropods." Metatarsus longer than femur. Humerus/femur ~1.38.
- Mosaic: pelvis with *Anchiornis*-like ischium and "troodontid"-like pubic apron; feet mixing *Archaeopteryx*, troodontid and dromaeosaurid traits. "The unique combination of postcranial features shared with early paravians ... shows how deeply the avialan phylogeny has been affected by evolutionary mosaicism. That, in turn, partly explains the controversy about the interrelationships among these early-diverging paravians."
- Placement: within Anchiornithidae, "resolved as the earliest diverging group of the Avialae"; *Archaeopteryx* sister to the rest of Avialae. Robust to parsimony and Bayesian tip-dating.
- Not built for flight: "metacarpal II and its associate digit are gracile, rather than being robust as in Anchiornis ... where they serve as the attachment for primary remiges; and metacarpals I and II are strongly ginglymoid ... indicative of a high degree of flexion (which would aid prey capture ...)".
- Morphospace: with all limbs or forelimbs, *Fujianvenator* sits with *Archaeopteryx* and *Anchiornis* "in an intermediate position." With hindlimbs only, "Fujianvenator is located far away from all other theropods." The avialan body plan change was "largely driven by the forelimb."
- Cursoriality score "substantially higher than that of other non-avialan theropods, including troodontids and tyrannosaurids." But "a wading ecology is equally possible ... the ecology of Fujianvenator remains an open question." Their summary: "an agile predator or a wader living in a swamp-like environment."
- Zhenghe Fauna: "more than 100 reptile fossils ... in our month of fieldwork," dominated by teleosts, turtles, choristoderes; dated 148-150 Ma.

Caution: the specimen lacks the skull and the feet are "poorly preserved," which is why runner vs wader can't be settled. The anchiornithid-as-avialan placement is one current hypothesis; other analyses put anchiornithids as troodontids or basal paravians (see [Sorkin 2020](sorkin-2020.md) for a survey).

Crop (b) and (c) separately.
