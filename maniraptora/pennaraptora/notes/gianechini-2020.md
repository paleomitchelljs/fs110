# Gianechini, Ercoli & Díaz-Martínez 2020, "Differential locomotor and predatory strategies of Gondwanan and derived Laurasian dromaeosaurids"

Gianechini, Ercoli & Díaz-Martínez. *Journal of Anatomy* 236:772-797 (2020). doi 10.1111/joa.13153.

Files: `papers/JOA-236-772.pdf` and `papers/Journal of Anatomy - 2020 - Gianechini - ...pdf` (same article, two downloads). 26 pages. Read the abstract, introduction, discussion and conclusion; methods and results skimmed.

## Claim

- Two phylogenetic PCAs: hindlimb bone lengths; pedal phalanx lengths. Unenlagiines, eudromaeosaurs, other theropods and living birds.
- Unenlagiines plot with "taxa with long tibiae, as well as long and slender metatarsi"; eudromaeosaurs with "shorter tibiae, and shorter and wider metatarsi."
- Reading: eudromaeosaurs had a short second phalanx on digit II and hinge-like joints, which "possibly allowed eudromaeosaurs to exert a great gripping strength and hunt large prey." Unenlagiines' slender, partly pinched metatarsus "possibly gave them greater cursorial capacities," and a longer phalanx for "fast movements of this digit to hunt smaller and elusive prey."
- Hedged throughout ("possibly"); form-function inference from proportions, no muscle or bone-strength modelling.

Not in the deck. It treats unenlagiines as dromaeosaurids, the placement Agnolín 2019 rejects, so it could sit beside the Recoding slide, or support a diet/predation line for dromaeosaurids if one is added.
