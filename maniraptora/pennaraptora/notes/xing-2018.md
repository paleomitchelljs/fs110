# Xing et al. 2018, "Multiple parallel deinonychosaurian trackways from a diverse dinosaur track assemblage of the Lower Cretaceous Dasheng Group of Shandong Province, China"

Xing, Lockley, Guo, Klein, Zhang J., Zhang L., Persons, Romilio, Tang & Wang X. *Cretaceous Research* 90:40-55 (2018). doi 10.1016/j.cretres.2018.04.005.

File: `papers/xingetal2018.pdf`, 16 pages. Read the abstract, the didactyl-track sections (4.1), discussion of Jehol comparisons and conclusions; the sauropod, ornithopod and bird track sections skimmed. Figures viewed.

## Claim

- Houmotuan tracksite, Dasheng Group, Shandong: the 14th site in the group, ~300 tracks on five levels, about 14 ichnogenera.
- Four trackways (HMT-T22 to T25) of small two-toed tracks, average 7.4 cm long, referred to cf. *Menglongpus*. "Four parallel trackways ... are differentiated from larger tridactyl (grallatorid) tracks by size, morphology and direction of travel, which suggests passage of a social group."
- Spacing: "Intertrackway spacing ... ranges from 30 to 88 cm," average ~53 cm. "Trackways HMT-23 and HMT-24 crossed over towards their ends."
- Identification is argued, not obvious: digit II is "either lacking or too indistinct"; they rule out an injured foot ("monodactyly" recurs across trackways) and a tridactyl maker with poor preservation (other theropod tracks on the same surface are clearly three-toed). Digit III/IV length ratio 2.3, more than in *Menglongipus* (1.8).
- Size: track lengths match the Jehol deinonychosaurs (all under ~2 m body length), which have body fossils where Shandong has none.

A second site with parallel two-toed trackways, so Li 2008 is not the only case. Weaker than Li's in two ways: the two-toed reading rests partly on absence of a toe trace, and two trackways cross, which is not the even spacing Li used as the group test.

Pairs with [Li et al. 2008](li-2008.md), [Roach & Brinkman 2007](roach-brinkman-2007.md), [Xing et al. 2024](xing-2024.md).
