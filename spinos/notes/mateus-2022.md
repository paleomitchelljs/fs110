# Mateus & Estraviz-López 2022, "A new theropod dinosaur from the early cretaceous (Barremian) of Cabo Espichel, Portugal: Implications for spinosaurid evolution"

*PLoS ONE* 17(2):e0262614. File: `spinos/papers/mateus-2022-iberospinus.pdf`. Read: abstract, captions. Figs 2, 3 viewed.

## Claim

Portuguese remains ML 1190, earlier called *Baryonyx walkeri*, are a new genus and species, *Iberospinus natarioi*. Papo Seco Fm, early Barremian. Diagnosed partly by "a single Meckelian foramen."
