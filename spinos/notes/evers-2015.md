# Evers et al. 2015, "A reappraisal of the morphology and systematic position of the theropod dinosaur Sigilmassasaurus from the 'middle' Cretaceous of Morocco"

*PeerJ* 3:e1323. File: `spinos/papers/evers-2015-sigilmassasaurus.pdf`, 101 pages. Read: abstract, neotype discussion, caption list. Figs 1, 23 viewed.

## Claim

*Sigilmassasaurus* is a valid spinosaurid, separate from *Spinosaurus*. The neotype designation is doubtful.

## Facts and quotes

- On the neotype's history: the remains were bought, and the authors only later found the collector. "there is no information on the original association of the remains, nor is there additional information on the locality."
- "As most vertebrates in the 'Kem Kem beds' are found in multitaxonomic bone beds ..., such information is crucial."
- "local collectors in Morocco and fossil dealers often sort remains acquired from different sources according to fitting size and morphology (U Leonhardt, pers. comm., 2015)." A personal communication, not a demonstration about this specimen.
- Fair to Ibrahim: "the vertebrae included in this specimen are of matching size," and so are the left and right femora and tibiae.
- "at least parts of the specimen described as 'Spinosaurus B' ... can be referred to a spinosaurid close to Sigilmassasaurus."
- Authors' examination of the neotype: "casts of the 'neotype'."
- Reply: [Ibrahim 2020 ZooKeys](ibrahim-2020-zookeys.md) rejects all of this.
