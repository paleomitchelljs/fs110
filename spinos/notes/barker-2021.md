# Barker et al. 2021, "New spinosaurids from the Wessex Formation (Early Cretaceous, UK) and the European origins of Spinosauridae"

*Scientific Reports* 11:19340. Barker, Hone, Naish, Cau, Lockwood, Foster, Clarkin, Schneider & Gostling. File: `spinos/papers/barker-2021-wessex-spinosaurids.pdf`. Read: abstract, description summary, captions. Figs 1, 7 viewed.

## Claim

Two new baryonychines from one formation on the Isle of Wight, and a tree suggesting spinosaurids began in Europe. Hone and Cau are authors, both recur in the debate.

## Facts

- *Ceratosuchops inferodios* and *Riparovenator milnerae*, Chilton Chine. "Both include partial skulls."
- Ceratosuchopsini includes *Suchomimus*.

## Figures

**Fig 1 (PDF p2): SKELETAL SILHOUETTES, TWO SPECIES.** *Ceratosuchops* (rear), *Riparovenator* (front), "White bones represent recovered elements." "The arrangement of the elements in the caudal series is estimated." Whole bodies are inferred.

**Fig 7 (PDF p9): TIME-CALIBRATED TREE.** Includes *Spinosaurus* type, "Spinosaurus B," MSNM V4047, FSAC KK11888 and *Sigilmassasaurus* as separate tips, which keeps the identity question open. ML 1190 still shown as cf. *Baryonyx*, before [Mateus 2022](mateus-2022.md).
