# Smart & Sakamoto 2024, "Using linear measurements to diagnose the ecological habitat of Spinosaurus"

*PeerJ* 12:e17544. File: `spinos/papers/peerj-17544.pdf`. Read: abstract, Fig 1.

- Linear skull measures, size-adjusted, cluster analysis. "terrestrial ecologies to be significantly distinct from both semi- and fully aquatic ecologies, the latter two were not significantly different."
- "Spinosaurids (including Spinosaurus) plotted away from theropods in morphospace."
- Conflicts with [Hone & Holtz 2021](hone-holtz-2021.md), whose skull PCA "fails to clearly discriminate," and [Sereno 2026](sereno-2026.md), who says skull-only work can't separate them.

**Fig 1 (PDF p4).** Morphospace with skull silhouettes, measuring diagram. Composite skulls, not specimens.
