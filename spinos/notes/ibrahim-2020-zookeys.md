# Ibrahim et al. 2020, "Geology and paleontology of the Upper Cretaceous Kem Kem Group of eastern Morocco"

*ZooKeys* 928:1-216. Ibrahim, Sereno, Varricchio, Martill, Dutheil, Unwin, Baidder, Larsson, Zouhri & Kaoukaya. File: `spinos/papers/ibrahim-2020b-kem-kem-monograph.pdf`. Read: *Spinosaurus* section, PDF p143-158. Same year as, and different from, the Nature tail paper ([Ibrahim 2020](ibrahim-2020.md)). Ibrahim and Sereno are still on one paper here, two years before they split.

## Claim

FSAC-KK 11888 is one associated individual, the neotype stands, and there is one large Kem Kem spinosaurid.

## Facts and quotes

- Site: "not a poorly cemented channel lag but rather a well cemented, massive sandstone preserving the remains of a single partially articulated individual." "There are no duplicate bones." "a portion of the manual digits ... was found in articulation."
- "A more plausible concern ... is actually opposite": commercial collectors break up associated skeletons rather than assemble them.
- Holotype: "In 1944 all of the spinosaurid bones described by Stromer (1915, 1934, 1936) were destroyed in war." "All that remains are Stromer's lithographic plates (1915: pl. 1, 2) and two photographs."
- "The bones of the holotype were collected by Richard Markgraf in 1912."
- "Spinosaurus B," BSPG 1922 X 45: "Given the small size of the limb bones versus the vertebrae, Stromer regarded the material as pertaining to two individuals." "Overlap with the holotype ... is limited to two strongly pinched, spool-shaped dorsal centra ... and teeth that were never figured."
- "The current controversy over the number of spinosaurids in the Kem Kem Group originates with the non-overlap between these two Egyptian specimens."
- Russell 1996 "designated a single mid cervical vertebra (CMN 50791) as the holotype of a new species" (*S. maroccanus*), and *Sigilmassasaurus* on a single vertebra.
- Neural spine height "up to ten times greater than centrum height."
