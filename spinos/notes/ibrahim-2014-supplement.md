## Facts and quotes

- Discovery: "was made by a local collector in 2008." "Subsequently, the collector was relocated, and he took three of us (NI, DMM, SZ) to the excavation site. In 2013, complete excavation of the site."
- "We recorded what could be recalled about the positioning of the bones from the local collector who found and excavated the specimen in 2008."
- Neotype: "a partial subadult skeleton including fragmentary remains of the skull and portions of the axial column, manus, pelvic girdle, and hind limbs."

## Figures

**Fig S2 (PDF p23): BONES, TWO FINDS AT ONE SCALE.** "Neotype material (digital elements, in gray) scaled to the same size as material of Spinosaurus B." Femur, distal caudal, cervicodorsal, pedal bones, dorsal, tibia. "A, D and F are 1:1 comparisons, with the same bones preserved in both associated skeletons."

**Fig S3 (PDF p25): SKELETON, COMPOSITE, COLOR-CODED.** "Color coding: red, neotype (FSAC-KK 11888); orange, Stromer's bones; yellow, isolated bones from the Kem Kem; green, surrogate bones modeled or taken from the spinosaurids Suchomimus, Baryonyx, Irritator or Ichthyovenator; blue, inferred bones from adjacent bones. A red dot below the posterior dorsal centra shows the approximate position of the center of mass. Model created by Tyler Keillor, with technical assistance of Lauren Conroy and Erin Fitzgerald."
