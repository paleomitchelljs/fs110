# Hone & Holtz 2019, comment on Arden et al.

"Comment on: Aquatic adaptation in the skull of carnivorous dinosaurs (Theropoda: Spinosauridae) and the evolution of aquatic habits in spinosaurus." *Cretaceous Research* 134:104152. Online 31 May 2019, volume dated 2022. File: `spinos/papers/1-s2.0-S0195667119300485-main.pdf`. Read in part.

- "the very term 'semi-aquatic' is poorly defined in the literature."
- Raised orbital rims also in "*Gallimimus*," "*Skorpiovenator*," "*Thescelosaurus*," "*Edmontosaurus*," "*Pentaceratops*," "*Herrerasaurus*," "none of which are suggested to have had aquatic affinities."
- Nares "are not dorsally located as would be expected of an animal living in water." "spinosaurs could not get to a crocodile-like or hippo-like posture with only the nares and eyes exposed."
- "Some spinosaurs may have spent extensive time in water, but the data to support this is currently insufficient."
