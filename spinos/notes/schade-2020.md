# Schade et al. 2020, "Neuroanatomy of the spinosaurid Irritator challengeri"

*Scientific Reports* 10:9259. Schade, Rauhut & Evers. File: `spinos/papers/schade-2020-irritator-neuroanatomy.pdf`, 9 pages. Read: discussion, Fig 1.

## Claim

The inner ear of SMNS 58022 suggests fast pitch-down head movements and a snout held down.

## Quotes

- "The skull table and lateral semicircular canal plane are strongly angled to one another, suggesting a downward angling of approximately 45° of the snout."
- "consistent with fast, downward snatching movements in the act of predation, such as are needed for piscivory."
- Their own caution: the approach of inferring head posture from the canal "has been criticized," since living birds deviate from a horizontal canal by up to about 20° either way.
- "direct evidence for spinosaurid diets indicates a mix, or opportunistic behaviour."
