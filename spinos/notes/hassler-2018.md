# Hassler et al. 2018, "Calcium isotopes offer clues on resource partitioning among Cretaceous predatory dinosaurs"

*Proc. R. Soc. B* 285:20180197. Hassler, Martin, Amiot, Tacail, Godet, Allain & Balter. File: `spinos/papers/rspb20180197.pdf`. Read: abstract, Fig 4, discussion near it.

## Facts

- Tooth enamel, Gadoufaoua (Niger) and Kem Kem (Morocco). Gadoufaoua's spinosaurid is *Suchomimus* country, so this isn't a direct *Spinosaurus* result. "Kem Kem spinosaurids are also strongly depleted in heavy Ca."
- "the predators of Gadoufaoua which had the most piscivorous-oriented diet," but "their diet was potentially a mix between fishes and herbivorous dinosaurs."
- The spinosaurid interval runs 0-100%, so it is a wide answer.
