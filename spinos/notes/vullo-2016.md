# Vullo et al. 2016, "Convergent evolution of jaws between spinosaurid dinosaurs and pike conger eels"

*Acta Palaeontologica Polonica* 61(4):825-828. Vullo, Allain & Cavin. File: `spinos/papers/vullo-2016-pike-conger.pdf`. Read in part.

Shared: "a mediolaterally compressed, elongated rostrum, a terminal 'rosette' bearing enlarged teeth in both upper and lower jaws, and a notch posterior to the premaxillary 'rosette'." Calls spinosaurs "recently claimed to have been semi-aquatic."

[Sereno 2026](sereno-2026.md) rejects it: "could not be more different."

**Fig 1 (PDF p2).** Diagrams: *Conger* to Muraenesocidae, *Dubreuillosaurus* to Spinosauridae. Schematic skulls, no specimen numbers.
