# Kellner et al. 2011, "A new dinosaur (Theropoda, Spinosauridae) from the Cretaceous (Cenomanian) Alcântara Formation, Cajual Island, Brazil"

*Anais da Academia Brasileira de Ciências* 83(1):99-108. File: `spinos/papers/kellner-2011-oxalaia.pdf`. Read: abstract, holotype. Fig 1 viewed.

- *Oxalaia quilombensis*. "based on the anterior part of a snout and a fragment of a maxilla."
- "Holotype: Fused premaxillae of a very large individual (ca. 12-14m, 5-7 tons)," MN 6117-V. The size comes from a snout tip. Referred left maxilla MN 6119-V.
- "one functional tooth followed by two replacement teeth."

Fig 1 (PDF p3) is two photos of the holotype. The deck uses the sharper four-view [Sales & Schultz 2017](sales-schultz-2017.md) Fig 6.
