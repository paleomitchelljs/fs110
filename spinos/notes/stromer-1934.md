# Stromer 1934, "Wirbeltier-Reste der Baharije-Stufe. 13. Dinosauria"

*Abhandlungen der Bayerischen Akademie der Wissenschaften*, N.F. 22. File: `spinos/papers/stromer-1934-dinosauria.pdf`, 82 pages. German with an OCR layer. Plate legend read. Rest sampled.

## Facts

- Legend to Doppeltafel I: "Alle vom Skelettfunde Nr. 1922 X 45, mit Sicherheit oder Wahrscheinlichkeit zu Spinosaurus B gehörigen, hier abgebildeten Knochen sind auf ein Drittel natürlicher Größe verkleinert." "With certainty or probability," in his own words.
- Bones figured: front neck centrum, trunk vertebrae, tail vertebrae, rib, chevrons, gastralia, ilium fragments, femur end, right tibia, pedal phalanges, claw.
- From [Ibrahim 2020 ZooKeys](ibrahim-2020-zookeys.md): recovered by Markgraf at one outcrop, "Given the small size of the limb bones versus the vertebrae, Stromer regarded the material as pertaining to two individuals." Overlap with the holotype "is limited to two strongly pinched, spool-shaped dorsal centra ... and teeth that were never figured."

## Figures

**Tafel I (PDF p80): LITHOGRAPHS, ONE FIND, POSSIBLY TWO ANIMALS.** BSPG 1922 X 45, "*Spinosaurus* B," individual bones at one-third natural size. Not a skeleton. Tafel II, III are other taxa (*Bahariasaurus*, others).
